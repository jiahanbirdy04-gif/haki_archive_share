Lost in the Stars

消失的她

C-Drama · Movie · China · 2023

⭐⭐⭐⭐☆1M/10

Overview

The film follows He Fei, whose wife Li Muzi mysteriously disappears during their anniversary trip to the fictional Southeast Asian country of Barlandia. When a woman suddenly appears claiming to be Muzi, He Fei insists that she isn't his wife. But the woman possesses Muzi's passport, photographs, memories, and personal details. With nobody believing him, He Fei hires lawyer Chen Mai to help prove that the woman is an impostor. What initially appears to be a mysterious identity-theft conspiracy becomes increasingly complicated as Chen Mai and He Fei uncover evidence, encounter staged dangers, and become trapped in what seems like an elaborate criminal operation. Then comes the massive twist: almost everything has been staged. The woman, police officer, surgeon, mercenary, and even the photographer are actors. Chen Mai is actually Shen Man, Muzi's best friend and a theater director. The entire elaborate production was created to make He Fei confess what happened to Muzi. The truth is devastating: He Fei murdered Muzi while they were scuba diving because he wanted her money and had gambling debts. Muzi was preparing to divorce him, but she was actually pregnant with their child and had not intended to leave him. Shen Man's elaborate "theater" was ultimately an act of revenge and justice for her best friend.

My Review

This movie was damn good. The plot twist has a plot twist. SHIT. 😭 At first, I thought I was watching a mystery about a missing wife and some kind of identity-stealing organization. Then another thing happened, and another, and another. Every time I thought I understood what was happening, the movie basically said "Nope." The biggest thing I took from this movie is that gambling is not a good thing. He Fei's gambling addiction and debts became one of the reasons everything went this far. He became so desperate to solve his financial problems that he was willing to betray and eventually kill the person he was supposed to love. And that's probably the most painful part for me: betrayal hurts the most when it comes from the person you never expected to betray you—or from the person you loved the most. Muzi trusted He Fei. She married him. She was even pregnant with their child. And he still chose money over her life. The movie really made me think about "Gaano ka magmahal?" How much can you actually love someone? Because sometimes people say they love someone, but their actions prove otherwise. The saddest revelation was that Muzi wasn't even planning to divorce him because she was pregnant. He killed her thinking she was going to leave him, when she was actually carrying their child and was still willing to give their relationship another chance. That made He Fei's betrayal even more disgusting. And then there's Shen Man. I honestly admire how far she went for her best friend. She didn't just investigate what happened to Muzi. She created an entire theater production specifically to make the man responsible for her death reveal the truth. The whole thing was insane. The actors, the fake police, the fake hospital, the fake murder, the fake conspiracy—it was basically one massive performance. And the fact that she did all of that for the friend she lost makes it even more powerful. I also loved how the movie kept making me question what was real. It wasn't just a mystery about "Where is Muzi?" It slowly became "What the hell is actually happening?" And the ending was brutal. Shen Man telling He Fei that by killing Muzi, he also killed his own child was probably one of the most painful parts. Overall, 10/10. The movie was suspenseful, shocking, heartbreaking, and satisfying. It reminded me that sometimes the person closest to you can be the person you should fear the most. And seriously... the plot twist had a plot twist. 😭
